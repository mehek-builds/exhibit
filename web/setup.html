Set up Exhibit

An agent that builds your O-1A / EB-1A evidence binder from what already happens in your inbox, calendar, GitHub and LinkedIn.

Not legal advice. Exhibit organizes evidence for an immigration attorney. It never decides eligibility, never files anything with USCIS, and never contacts an attorney or a recommender without your approval. Nothing is sent, and nothing is added to your binder, without you.

What Exhibit asks for, in plain words

Gmail send is not requested here. It is a separate permission you grant later, and it only ever sends email you approved: a letter request you replied APPROVE to, or a digest to yourself. Until you grant it, Exhibit sends nothing.
